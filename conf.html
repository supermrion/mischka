May I Court You?

For I find within myself a desire to love someone like you. A subtle urge to serenade you with my songs of affection, to adore you, and to leave no part of my heart unspoken. I see the charming presence of yours that draws me closer, and the beauty of a woman you are that compels me to know and understand you beyond any other’s. Because the further I reach to know you, the more I find myself itching to know more. And what began as admiration had grown into something I can no longer call as the same.
I do not ask this lightly.
To be your suitor is not to just place a bouquet of flowers in your hands or to write you letters when the moment calls for them, but to make my intentions known, to let my affection known, not hidden, and to passionately earn and yearn for the place I am looking forward to have in your life. I want to approach you with sincerity and intention, for I know best to prove them to you in both words and actions. I want to listen to you when you speak, and listen to you when you don’t, for I also want to learn by heart what matters to you, makes you happy, makes you sad, angry, calm, brings you warmth, brings you joy. And I want to be constantly present even when there is nothing convenient to say.
I wish to be someone who understands you.
Someone who knows the little things that make you feel the way you want to feel. The little things that make you smile, the things you deeply care for, the dreams you hope to reach, and even the things you yourself may not be ready to show to anyone. I want to know the woman behind the beauty that first caught my attention, you, and I want to appreciate every part of you, whether good or bad, with the same passion with which I admire you.

And I'll never be afraid to show you.

For I would never be ashamed to let the people around us know how dearly I admire you. I would speak of you with pride and hide no love I have for you.
Not because I wish to possess you, nor because I need everyone to know that you are mine, but because I am to never feel the need to conceal loving someone as charming as you.
I would let my affection for you be known, not for the sake of being heard, but through the assurance with which I would speak your name. I would tell them of the person who has so graudually become important to me, of the smile I find myself thrilling over, and of the presence I have come to look forward to. I would never hesitate to introduce you with pride, to stand beside you with passion, or to let my admiration show whenever your name is spoken. If one is to ask me who I had grown so fond of, I would have no reason to hide the answer.
For it would be you.
For I do not want to love you only when no one is looking.
I want to be able to look at you in a room full of people and still let my eyes find you. I want to speak of you without hesitation, to support you with no exception, and to let you know that I am proud to admire you.
And if fate had brought our paths together, perhaps it's ours to decide whether we shall walk the same direction together. I do not ask for decaying promises; not some oath for tomorrow might undo. I ask only whether you would allow me to know you more, and this time, with intention, to walk beside you with care, and to let my love prove itself not in the loudest declarations, but in the constancy of my actions towards you.

For when I am near you, a greater part of me comes to life. Chivalry never ceases to awaken as though it had been waiting for your presence. And so, Mischka, having you found where I never thought to look, I must ask, with all my will, may I court you?

For I cannot look away
now that I've seen you.
