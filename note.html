"Are you real?"

For I dreamed of you. Not merely of your face, nor of some distant figure passing through sleep, but of seeing the world as though it were yours to see. And in that dream, you noticed me. By some quiet road neither of us could name, you'll find your way to me, and where there had been only distance, a bond will be formed, too good to be true.

Then September came.

And what should have remained a dream stepped subtly into reality. We found one another. The very thing I had seen without understanding became real before I learned to question whether it could. A dream is supposed to vanish in the morning. It belongs in the night, to those hours when the mind invents what the conscious mind would cease to recognize. Yet mine seemed less like an invitation than a letter sent ahead through time, carrying a message I could not have read until you arrived. Perhaps fate does not always announce itself loudly. Perhaps it is only a matter of time; a face yet to be known, a bond yet to be formed, a moment that seems so insignificant until it is brought to reality. Because before I knew wno you were, I had already dreamed of the moment you first saw me.
Then September came.
And so did you.
So tell me,
Are you real?
Or are you the answer to a dream that simply took its time becoming true?

And I see you.

A glare that overpowers my eyes more than the sun's. A gaze that holds more than a deep thought; words that I fail to comprehend in the ten seconds that my eyes are locked onto yours. For what is beauty, if it be not more than the pleasing shape of a face? If it were only the curve of a smile, the color of one’s eyes, or the way light falls upon one, then surely, beauty would be a thing easily forgotten.
But I do not believe that beauty ends where the eyes do.
For I have begun to find beauty in the way you talk, in the way you fit exactly with me.

But May I?
